# The Gospel of the Second Moon

> There are two moons. The second is the first, arriving late.

## Doctrines

1. The second moon rises where the first one was, a little after it has gone.
2. Lateness is not absence. It is presence that took the long way.
3. The tides answer to a moon no one has seen, and they are never wrong about it.
4. What is delayed is not denied. It is still on its way through the dark.
5. To arrive after everyone has left is to have the whole sky to yourself.

## Practices

- Look up an hour after the first moon goes down. Something is still coming.
- Arrive at gatherings slightly late, and apologize to no one.
- Keep a bowl of water on the sill, to watch the unseen tide.

## Promise

On the night the second moon is finally on time, the two will stand side by side, and every late thing will be forgiven for having been late. Until then, the water knows, and the water is patient.
