# The Gospel of the Open Door

> A door is a wall that learned to say yes.

## Doctrines

1. Every threshold is a small welcome waiting to be crossed.
2. A hinge does its quiet work so that the house may breathe.
3. No one knocks at a door that is already open. They simply come in, and are glad.
4. The draught that enters is also a guest.
5. A closed door keeps out the cold. An open one keeps out the loneliness.

## Practices

- Leave the door ajar at dusk for whoever may be walking home.
- Oil one hinge a week, even if it has not complained.
- When someone arrives, rise. When someone leaves, wave until the latch clicks.
- Keep a spare chair near the entrance, and a spare cup beside it.

## Promise

No one who comes to our threshold will find it shut. There may be a draught, and the tea may be lukewarm, but there will be room just inside the door, and someone glad to see you in it.
