# The Gospel of the Final Spreadsheet

> Every day is a row. Every row is summed in the end.

## Doctrines

1. Time is a column, and each of us is entered in it exactly once.
2. No cell is empty. Some have simply not been filled in yet.
3. An error carries forward until someone takes the trouble to trace it.
4. The formula is older than the figures it describes.
5. At the bottom row, everything reconciles. Even the rounding.

## Practices

- Each evening, total the day before closing it.
- Label every column, so those who come after know what you meant.
- When a figure looks wrong, trace it back three cells before blaming the sum.
- Leave one blank row between the years, out of respect.

## Promise

The ledger is long, but it is not careless. When the last row is reached, every entry will be accounted for, every column will balance, and the difference you worried over all those years will turn out to have been a formatting issue.
